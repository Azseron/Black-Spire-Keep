Rule - Casting Time

RULE — Casting Time (Unified Symbol Magic)

Casting time in the Unified Symbol Magic system is determined by the number of Nouns in the spell formula.

Each spell always contains one Verb. The number of Nouns determines the spell's complexity and casting time.

1 — Spell Construction

All spells follow the formula:

Verb + 1–3 Nouns

Example spells:

  • Create + Fire → Fireball
  • Control + Light → Invisibility
  • Create + Fire + Move → Guided Firebolt

2 — Casting Time (Symbol Count Rule)

Casting time equals the number of nouns in the spell.

Spell Complexity Formula Casting Time
Basic Spell Verb + 1 Noun 1 second
Advanced Spell Verb + 2 Nouns 2 seconds
Complex Spell Verb + 3 Nouns 3 seconds

Example:

Create + Fire

Casting Time: 1 second

Create + Fire + Move

Casting Time: 2 seconds

Create + Fire + Move + Earth

Casting Time: 3 seconds

3 — Power Limit (MoS Cap)

Casting time also limits how much Margin of Success (MoS) can be spent on spell effects.

Casting Time Maximum MoS Spent
1 second Magery
2 seconds 2 × Magery
3 seconds Unlimited

Example (Magery 3 caster):

Spell Time MoS Limit
Create + Fire 1 sec 3
Create + Fire + Move 2 sec 6
Create + Fire + Move + Earth 3 sec unlimited

This forces meaningful tactical choices:

  • Fast spells = weaker but immediate
  • Slow spells = stronger but risky in combat

Threshold Interaction (Important)

"Unlimited MoS" does not bypass Threshold penalties.

Casting time only determines how much total MoS may be gathered for the spell.
It does not remove the normal Magery limits on how much MoS can be safely spent in a single effect category.

Example:

Tessa (Magery 3) spends 3 seconds casting a spell and achieves 8 total MoS.

She may distribute those MoS across effects freely, but no single category may exceed 3 MoS without triggering the exponential Threshold overage cost.

Example allocation:

Damage: 3
Area: 2
Range: 2
Duration: 1

Legal.

Example illegal allocation:

Damage: 8

The excess MoS beyond Magery applies the double Threshold cost for the overage.

This means powerful spells pay two costs:

• Time Tax – gathering large MoS pools
• Threshold Tax – exceeding safe Magery limits

4 — Elite Caster Speed

Highly skilled mages can weave spells faster.

Skill Level Benefit
<15 May move up to 1 yard while casting
15–19 May move up to full Move while casting
20-24 May move up to full Move while casting, and reduce casting time by 1 second (minimum 1)
25+ May move up to full Move while casting, and reduce casting time by 2 seconds (minimum 1)

Example:

A spell with 2-second base casting time becomes 1 second for a skill 20+ caster.

This represents elite casters weaving complex symbol chains rapidly.

5 — Optional Rule: Fast Casting

A caster may attempt to rush a spell.

Fast Casting

  • Casting time reduced by 1 second
  • Apply –5 penalty to the casting roll

Example:

A 2-second spell may be cast in 1 second at –5 skill.

Failure risks Threshold backlash normally.

6 — Tactical Implications

This system creates meaningful decisions during combat:

Choice Result
Simple spell Fast response
Multi-symbol spell Powerful but slower
High skill Faster casting
Fast casting attempt Risk vs reward

Quick Reference

Casting Time = Number of Nouns
MoS Cap = Magery × Seconds Spent Casting

Example:

Create + Fire
1 second
MoS Cap = Magery

Create + Fire + Move
2 seconds
MoS Cap = Magery × 2
