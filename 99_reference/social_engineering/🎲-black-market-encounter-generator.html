Black Market Encounter Generator

Chicago – Criminal Core Underworld

Use this generator when:

  • A Hunter attempts to sell cores illegally.
  • A broker must be introduced quickly.
  • A deal feels too smooth.
  • You need tension without combat.
  • You want structured Social Engineering play.

This tool supports fast improvisation during live sessions.

Step 1 – Who Do They Meet?

Roll 1d6 or choose.

1 – Street Fence (Tier 1)

  • Nervous, opportunistic.
  • Slightly overpays to hook new sellers.
  • High betrayal risk.
  • Possibly flipped by DPS.

2 – Independent Broker (Tier 2)

  • Professional and calm.
  • Tests authenticity carefully.
  • Interested in repeat business.
  • Mid-level criminal connections.

3 – Syndicate Representative (Tier 3)

  • Polite but quiet.
  • Silent security nearby.
  • Evaluates the sellers more than the core.
  • May offer exclusivity.

4 – Academic Middleman

  • Claims research purpose.
  • Too clean.
  • Possibly DPS-adjacent or cult-adjacent.
  • Asks technical questions.

5 – Rival Hunter Moving Cores

  • Recognizes the PCs.
  • Competitive.
  • May attempt partnership or undercut.
  • Could become recurring contact.

6 – Unexpected Third Party

  • Gang lieutenant interrupts.
  • Scanner chatter nearby.
  • Power flickers.
  • Someone else is watching.

Step 2 – Buyer Motivation

Roll 1d6.

1 – Pure Profit
Wants margin. Nothing personal.

2 – Territory Enforcement
Testing whether PCs respect boundaries.

3 – Information Gathering
Asks too many questions about Fire Opal.

4 – Recruitment Angle
Hints at larger operations.

5 – Ritual Demand
Specifically interested in undead/demon cores.

6 – Political Leverage
Interested in Ryan’s family or public visibility.

Step 3 – Complication Trigger

Roll secretly if tension is needed.

1 – Heat
Someone nearby recognizes the PCs.

2 – Underpayment Attempt
Buyer claims core is mana-scarred (-20%).

3 – Exclusivity Demand
Higher price offered in exchange for loyalty.

4 – Territory Conflict
“This corridor isn’t yours.”

5 – Core Resonance
A-core pulses or reacts unexpectedly.

6 – Clean
No complication. Smooth deal.

Step 4 – Social Mechanics (Quick Use)

Use these if running structured Social Engineering.

Finding a Buyer:

  • Streetwise (IQ-based)

Negotiation:

  • Merchant vs Merchant
  • or Fast-Talk vs Merchant

Lying About Grade:

  • Fast-Talk vs Merchant

Spotting Deception:

  • Merchant or Detect Lies

Intimidation:

  • Intimidation vs Will

Reaction Roll modifies:

  • Offer price
  • Respect level
  • Future access

Quick Price Adjustment

Base Black Market Price × Modifier:

Poor Reaction: ×0.75
Neutral Reaction: ×1.0
Good Reaction: ×1.25
Very Good Reaction: ×1.5

Critical Success:

  • Add Favor opportunity.

Critical Failure:

  • PCs marked unreliable.

Escalation Tracker (GM Only)

Track two hidden values:

Underworld Standing: -3 to +3
DPS Suspicion: 0 to 5

Each illegal sale:
+1 Underworld Standing
+1 DPS Suspicion (if sloppy or large transaction)

At Suspicion 3:

  • Audit triggered.

At Suspicion 5:

  • Investigation arc begins.

Fire Opal Spike Adjustment

Because the team cleared a spike dungeon:

Selling:

  • C-Core → Routine tension.
  • B-Core → Serious buyer interest.
  • A-Core → Tier 3 involvement guaranteed.

Do not escalate to crackdown immediately.
Let tension build gradually.

GM Guidance

  • Do not punish first offense instantly.
  • Escalate consequences slowly.
  • Criminal consequences are personal, not legal.
  • Reputation spreads slowly.
  • A-Rank cores are political events.
  • Money is powerful; leverage is stronger.
  • Always leave room for retreat.
