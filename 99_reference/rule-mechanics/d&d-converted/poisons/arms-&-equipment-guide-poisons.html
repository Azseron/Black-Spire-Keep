Alchemy: Some New Poisons

This post describes the conversions of poisons from Arms and Equipment Guide for D&D 3.0.

Aboleth Oil

A living victim that makes contact with aboleth oil, must make an immediate HT roll to resist. If they fail, 10 minutes later they lose their ability to breathe air and gain the ability to breathe water for margin of failure minutes. Being washed off with cool, fresh water during the onset period negates the effect.
Form: Contact Poison.
Cost: $690 (singular); $250 (5-batched).
Recipe: $69; 4 days; defaults to Poisons-3.
Statistics: Affliction 1 (HT; Advantage, Alternate Form (same template, but with Doesn’t Breathe (Gills; underwater only)); +150%; Contact Poison, -5%; Onset, 10 minutes, -10%; Terminal Condition, Cool, fresh water, -10%) [23].

Adlevine Extract
This poison is extracted from a tropical vine. A living victim who ingests the poison must make a HT roll after 10 minutes or suffer 1d-1 toxic damage. Every minute for 6 more cycles he must make another HT roll to resist or take the same amount of damage. After losing 1/2 HP to this poison, the victim gains a -4 penalty to IQ until he heals above this threshold.
Form: Ingested Poison.
Cost: $180 (singular); $65 (5-batched).
Recipe: $18; 1 day; defaults to Poisons-1.
Statistics: Toxic Attack 1d-1 (Cyclic, 1 minute, 6 cycles, Resistible, +120%; Ingested Poison, -85%; Onset, 10 minutes, -10%; Resistible, HT, -30%; Symptoms, 1/2 HP, -4 IQ, +80%) [6].

Brain Dust
This bitter-smelling dust causes the subjects to hallucinate. This poison flask can be thrown (range is STx3.5). Hitting a hard surface, it breaks, creating a cloud of poison gas in a 2-yard radius. The cloud persists for 10 seconds and may drift with the wind. Any creature that inhales the gas makes an HT roll to resist. On failure, the creature begins to hallucinate and gains a -2 penalty to IQ for margin of failure minutes.
Form: Grenade.
Cost: $1,080 (singular); $390 (5-batched).
