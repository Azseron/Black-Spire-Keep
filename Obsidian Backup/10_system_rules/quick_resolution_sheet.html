Core Roll

Roll 3d6 ≤ Effective Skill

Effective Skill = Base Skill + Modifiers

If skill is 16+, remember the Rule of 16 (resistances cap at 16 unless specified).

Margin of Success (MoS)

MoS = Skill – Roll

Use MoS to determine:

  • Speed (faster completion)
  • Quality (clean execution)
  • Extra effect (if system allows)
  • Reduced time or cost
  • Combat effects (if applicable)

Do not inflate magnitude unless explicitly stated.

Critical Results

Critical Success:

  • 3–4 always
  • 5 if skill 15+
  • 6 if skill 16+

Critical Failure:

  • 18 always
  • 17 if skill 15 or less
  • 16 if skill 10 or less

D&D DC → GURPS Conversion

D&D DC GURPS Modifier
10 +4
15 +0
18 –2
20 –3
21–22 –4
23–25 –5 to –6
26–30 –7 (up to –10 only if truly legendary)

For DC 25+, do not exceed –6 unless nearly impossible even for specialists.

Always judge narratively before applying penalty.

Complementary Skills

Success on related skill:
+1 (or +2 on strong success)

Failure:
No bonus (or possible –1 at GM discretion)

Limit stacking. Keep it sane.

Quick Contest

Both parties roll vs skill.

Higher MoS wins.

If tied:
Higher skill wins.
If still tied:
Roll again.

Time Modifiers (General Guide)

More time taken:
+1 to +4 (depending on scale)

Rushed:
–1 to –5

Extreme haste may require extra roll or risk.

Environmental Modifiers

Darkness:
–1 to –10 depending on severity

Poor footing:
–1 to –4

Distraction:
–1 to –3

High stress:
–1 to –2

Stack carefully. Avoid over-penalizing.

Combat Reminders

Aim:
+Acc after 1 second

All-Out Attack:
No defense

Shock:
–1 per HP lost (max –4)

When Not to Roll

Do not roll if:

  • Success is trivial
  • Failure adds nothing
  • No meaningful consequence exists
  • The character has overwhelming skill advantage

Rolling should introduce uncertainty.

GM Philosophy

Difficulty represents task challenge.

Skill represents character competence.

Gate Rank increases monster threat, not global skill penalties.

Keep rolls fast.
Keep penalties deliberate.
Keep MoS meaningful.
