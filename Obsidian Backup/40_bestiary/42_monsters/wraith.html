Monsters: Wraiths

“Wraith-5e.webp” could not be found.D&D wraiths are very different from GURPS Magic wraiths. These ones are just undead spirits that are only slightly different from the spectres. Dread wraiths, on the other hand, have an interesting lifesense ability.
