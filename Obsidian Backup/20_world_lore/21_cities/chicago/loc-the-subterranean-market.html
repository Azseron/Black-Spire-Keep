LOC - The Subterranean Market

🏮 The Subterranean Market

Also known as: "The Below," "Night Below," "Opal Street"

Overview

Hidden beneath Union Station in abandoned subway spurs and freight tunnels. It operates like a Korean Night Market—loud, crowded, steamy, and chaotic. It is the only place in Chicago where Hunters, criminals, and corporate spies mingle freely.

The Golden Rule: No fighting. Violence is punished by immediate execution by the Market Guard.

🧭 Layout & Atmosphere

  • Visuals: Plastic tarps, neon signs powered by mana batteries, steam from food stalls, glowing runes.
  • Smells: Gun oil, ozone, spicy street food, antiseptic ink.
  • Currency: US Dollars and Mana Cores (preferred for high-end gear).

🏪 Notable Stalls

The Strip (Main Corridor)

  • Food Stalls:
    • Mana Broth: Restores 1 FP ($50/bowl).
    • Cold Barley Tea: Suppresses Fear penalties for 1 hour.
  • The Demolitions Guy: Lena's contact. Never has a sign. Always "just packing up."
  • Information Brokers: Sit at folding tables with notebooks. Sell rumors for favors.

The Tattoo Tunnels (Side Passage)

  • Services: Illegal rune tattoos for Adept Hunters.
  • The Artist: A mute ink-master who claims to remember the "Old World."
  • Risk: Tattoos hurt. HT rolls required to avoid temporary shock.

The Deep Chambers (Restricted)

  • Dragovich's Office: Where NPC - Viktor Dragovich holds court.
  • Artifact Exchange: High-value trading. No cops allowed.

🔗 Connected NPCs

  • NPC - Viktor Dragovich (The Grey Wolf)
  • PC - Ryan Lockwood (frequents the gear stalls)
