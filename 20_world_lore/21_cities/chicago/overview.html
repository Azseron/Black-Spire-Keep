Chicago Overview — June 2025

Status Summary

Chicago in June 2025 is a contained crisis city. Life continues on the surface—jobs, traffic, summer festivals—but beneath that normalcy sits a growing lattice of Gates, quarantines, and quiet emergency powers. The public knows something is wrong. They do not know how wrong.

The city has not collapsed. It has adapted.

Public Narrative (What Civilians Believe)

  • A series of "containment incidents" tied to infrastructure failures, chemical leaks, and extremist activity.
  • Certain neighborhoods are restricted due to "structural instability" or "environmental hazards."
  • Increased federal presence is framed as counter-terrorism and disaster preparedness.
  • Viral footage of "animals" or "riots" is dismissed as hoaxes or misinterpretations.

Most people assume this is temporary.

The Reality (What Hunters Know)

  • Chicago is one of the highest Gate-density cities in North America.
  • Several Gates have already been neutralized; others remain dormant, sealed, or unacknowledged.
  • Residual mana is accumulating in underground spaces, old infrastructure, and abandoned buildings.
  • Monsters have escaped containment at least twice.
  • Cult activity is confirmed and escalating.

Hunters understand that Chicago is being managed, not saved.

Government Response

Federal

  • Emergency authority invoked under existing disaster and homeland security frameworks.
  • A dedicated task force operates quietly through shell agencies and utilities departments.
  • Data is classified aggressively; whistleblowers disappear into legal limbo.

State & City

  • City Hall cooperates under pressure.
  • Police and fire departments receive limited briefings.
  • First responders are told to withdraw from certain zones without explanation.

Officially, no supernatural threat exists.

Hunter Activity

Chicago hosts a large freelance hunter population, larger than average due to:

  • High Gate frequency
  • Abundant underground access points
  • Strong black-market infrastructure

Hunter culture in Chicago is:

  • Pragmatic
  • Suspicious
  • Highly networked
  • Less heroic, more professional

Reputation matters. Carelessness spreads quickly.

The Subterranean World

Beneath the streets exists a parallel ecosystem:

  • Abandoned subway spurs
  • Flooded service tunnels
  • Utility corridors
  • Forgotten bomb shelters

These spaces house:

  • The Subterranean Market
  • Hunter safehouses
  • Illegal clinics
  • Artifact brokers
  • Cult hideouts

This underground is alive, shifting nightly.

Factions Present

  • Government containment units (covert)
  • Independent hunter crews
  • Corporate interests (research & salvage)
  • Religious and esoteric cults
  • Information brokers and fixers

No single group is fully in control.

Gate Behavior

  • Gates appear unpredictably but favor older structures and underground spaces.
  • Some Gates destabilize after partial clearing.
  • Smaller "micro-Gates" open briefly, often ignored until damage is done.
  • Mana residue persists after closure, warping environments.

The Fire Opal Gate is one of the most dangerous confirmed examples.

Tone & Atmosphere

  • Summer heat mixed with unease
  • Increased police presence without explanation
  • Closed streets and construction zones everywhere
  • Rumors spreading faster than facts
  • A sense that something big is coming

Chicago feels watched.

Campaign Use

Use Chicago as:

  • A living hub between Gates
  • A pressure cooker for faction conflict
  • A place where choices have visible consequences
  • A city that reacts to hunter actions

Nothing resets between sessions.

Current Rumors (June 2025)

  • "A Gate opened under the lake. They sealed it, but something escaped."
  • "Someone is paying double for fire-aligned cores."
  • "A cult thinks a god is about to be reborn."
  • "The tunnels are changing. Old maps don’t work anymore."
  • "Hunters went in last week. No bodies came out."

Some of these are true.

GM Notes

Chicago is not a dungeon.

It is a system under stress.

The players are not saviors.

They are specialists operating in the cracks.
