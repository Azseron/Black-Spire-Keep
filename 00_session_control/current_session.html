Night Market Incident – Gunshot

During the A-Rank Core auction at the Subterranean Market, an unknown gunman opened fire and shot Ryan Lockwood in the head (24 damage).

Later investigation reveals:

• The gunman was hired by Viktor Dragovich to create a disturbance during the auction.
• The instructions were only to cause chaos, not to use lethal force or draw a weapon.
• The gunman panicked or escalated the situation beyond the original plan.

Market security reacted immediately.

Madam Opal's men intervened:

  • They subdued and drugged Dragovich's operative.
  • The man was removed from the area before ARD attention could be drawn.

The gunman escaped, fleeing the market in a black Chevrolet Impala.

Consequences

  • The auction continued after the disturbance.
  • Madam Opal quietly contained the situation to preserve the market’s neutrality.
  • Dragovich will likely be furious that his orders were not followed.
  • The gunman may now be a loose end for both Dragovich and Madam Opal.
