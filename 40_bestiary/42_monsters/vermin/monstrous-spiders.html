Monstrous Spiders

Monsters: Monstrous Spiders

Giant spiders can be considered classic dungeon monsters. I never knew that the monstrous spider entry in Monster Manual actually details two varieties of spiders - web-spinner and hunter. Here I present racial template for all types and sizes, because they can be used to represent different species, not age categories.
